How tracking works

Only target-language audio counts

  • Streaming subtitles never count as proof of spoken language.
  • If a site hides its selected audio, confirm the current video. After two matching confirmations, the tracker can offer to remember strongly related videos.
  • Fully manual language mode can count every supported video when you prefer to manage the language yourself.
  • Active time means you are using the browser; passive time means the video continues while you are away.
